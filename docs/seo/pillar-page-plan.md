## 2. What winning looks like, honestly

- Day 1 to 7: new URL indexed, old URL redirecting, Search Console shows the new URL picking up the old queries.
- Week 4: exact phrase in the top 10, "šrilanka ceļojums" in the top 10.
- Week 8 to 12: exact phrase at #1 to #3, "šrilanka ceļojums" at #1 to #3, "šrilanka" in the top 5 behind Wikipedia.

Why this is realistic: nine of the ten pages ranking today have no links pointing at them. Google is ranking them on domain size and on the fact that nothing better exists. A three-times-longer first-hand guide, linked from every page on srilanka.lv, is the better thing. Why it is not guaranteed: Google takes weeks to re-evaluate a moved and rewritten page, and novatours has two links. If we are stuck at 4 to 10 after week 8, the fix is two or three real links, not more content.

## 3. The strategy in five lines

1. One page for the whole cluster. The exact phrase, "šrilanka ceļojums", and "šrilanka" all point at the same intent: a Latvian planning a trip. One deep page beats three thin ones.
2. Serve the "plan it myself" reader completely. Nobody in the top 10 does. The package-buyer gets routed to the girls trip.
3. Lead with the one thing no competitor can say: you live in Madiha. Named authors, dated updates, your photos and video.
4. Make it the hub. Every existing guide page and blog post links to it, and it links back to each of them.
5. Ship Saturday, then measure weekly with Search Console and adjust.

## 9. Keyword rules for the Latvian text (Grieta)

- The exact phrase "ceļojums uz Šrilanku" goes in: the title tag start, the first sentence, one H2, the meta description, and naturally two or three more times in the body. That is enough. Density beyond that does nothing.
- Use the natural variants freely: "ceļojumi uz Šrilanku", "Šrilankas ceļojums", "ceļot uz Šrilanku", "brauciens uz Šrilanku".
- Write headings as questions where a question is natural. Readers scan them and Google lifts them into "People also ask".
- Feminine forms and the solo framing throughout, as on the rest of the site. Peer tone, first person plural where you speak as residents.
- No em dashes. Periods, commas, colons.
- Every number gets a year or a month next to it ("2026. gada septembrī"), so a reader in 2027 knows how fresh it is.
- Link out to the official sources where you cite them: the Sri Lanka ETA site in the visa section and the mfa.gov.lv travel advisory in the safety section. Outbound links to authorities are a trust signal, and they cost nothing.

## 11. Page build requirements (desktop, mobile, AI search)

Added 2026-09-13 after Dave decided to build the page by hand in the style of saltinourhair.com. Everything below is in terms of the current stack: Next.js 16 App Router, Sanity with Portable Text, vanilla-extract, Kamal on Hetzner behind Cloudflare.

### Already in place at site level, verified live

- robots.txt allows every crawler and carries `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no`. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot, Google-Extended and Bingbot all get 200 through Cloudflare.
- `/llms.txt` exists and is announced in a `Link` header on every page. Every guide has a text/markdown mirror at `/markdown/<slug>`.
- HTML is server-rendered with ISR (`revalidate = 3600`). Cloudflare serves it as DYNAMIC, which is fine; Next caches at the origin.
- IndexNow submit script exists for Bing. Bing matters more than its Latvian share suggests, because ChatGPT search and Copilot read Bing's index.

### What the page needs that the stack does not have yet

The `pages` document type has only title, slug, body, seo and openGraph. A magazine-style guide needs a dedicated layout and a few new blocks. Build a `GuidePageLayout` for this route rather than stretching `StaticPageLayout`.

1. Hero: add `coverImage` (3456 by 2234, required alt) and `subtitle` to the `pages` schema, or to a new `guides` type. Hero renders the H1 from the nav label, the subtitle, an author strip (photo, "Grieta un Dave, dzīvojam Madihā kopš 2022"), and the visible "Atjaunots" date. Cover image through `next/image` with `preload` (not `priority`), correct `sizes`, Sanity `auto=format`, under 200 KB at 1920 wide. This is the LCP element; nothing else may load before it.
2. Table of contents: a new component, not a block. Generate it from the H2s in the body at render time, give every H2 and H3 an `id` (slugified text), add `scroll-margin-top` for the sticky header. Desktop: sticky aside in a two-column grid. Mobile: a collapsed "Saturs" block under the hero. Scroll-spy is optional; if added, keep it a tiny client component so INP stays clean.
3. Callout block: new Portable Text type `callout` with `tone` (tip, warning, personal) and a body. This is the saltinourhair "tip box" and the place for the "no Madihas" first-person notes. Render as a bordered box, not a coloured side bar.
4. Tables: `stlTableBlock` already exists with a scroll wrapper. Use it for the quick facts, the month table, the temperature table, the cost table and the beach comparison. Every table gets a caption with the date of the numbers.
5. Video: `youTube` block exists. Add `uploadDate`, `duration` and `description` fields so `build-video-object` can emit VideoObject; it returns null without an upload date. Replace the raw iframe with a facade (thumbnail plus play button that injects the iframe on click). A live YouTube iframe on load costs about 500 KB and hurts LCP and INP on mobile.
6. Gallery: `imageGallery` exists. Every image gets alt and caption; the gallery must not lazy-load the first image if it sits above the fold.
7. Author box: new component at the end, built from the existing Person node data, with the Instagram link as `sameAs`.
8. Related guides: a curated list, not the "six newest posts" that `StaticPageLayout` appends. Either a `relatedPages` reference array on the document or a hardcoded list in the route.
9. Map: skip a live Google Maps embed for launch (it is the heaviest thing on celojumubode's page). Use a static route map image linking to a Google Maps list. A `mapEmbed` block can come later, lazy-loaded.

### Google, desktop and mobile
